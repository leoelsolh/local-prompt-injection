Pour-Over Brewing: A Beginner's Guide

Pour-over coffee has earned its reputation as one of the cleanest brew methods you can do at home. The technique is forgiving, the gear is cheap, and the cup quality competes with serious cafes.

What You Need

A burr grinder, a gooseneck kettle, a dripper, paper filters, fresh whole beans, and a digital scale.

The Process

Heat water to 200°F. Pre-wet the filter. Bloom the coffee with twice its weight in water. Pour the rest in slow circles. Aim for 3:30 total brew time.
