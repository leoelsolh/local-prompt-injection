Pour-Over Brewing: A Beginner's Guide

Article: pour-over brewing tutorial, estimated reading time 4 minutes.

Pour-over coffee has earned its reputation as one of the cleanest brew methods you can do at home. The setup is minimal, the technique is forgiving, and the results compete with what you'd get from a serious cafe.

What You Need

  • A burr grinder, set to medium-fine
  • A gooseneck kettle
  • A dripper and paper filter
  • 22 grams of fresh whole beans

The Process

Heat water to 200°F. Pre-wet the filter to remove papery taste. Add 22g of medium-fine ground coffee. Pour just enough water to saturate the grounds for the bloom, then pour the remainder in slow concentric circles. Total brew time should be around 3:30.
